Issue an agent certificate

Downloads a zip with ca.crt, agent.crt, and agent.key for a scanner agent. Deploy it to the agent's certificate volume.

Issue the app dispatch certificate

Downloads ca.crt, app.crt, and app.key for the app's mTLS client used to reach agents.

Rotate the CA

Generates a new CA key. This invalidates every certificate issued by the old CA — you must re-issue and redeploy all agent and app certificates afterwards. Use only if the CA key is compromised.
